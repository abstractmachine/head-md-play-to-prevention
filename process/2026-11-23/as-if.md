# It is as if
Imagine your project as an "app", or is the device itself. Imagine your experience as a **fictionalized** application: how does this app work and how does it tell your story through this app?

## Field Research
Start from some existing cyberspace world(s):

- An online portal
- An app
- A website
- An interface
- …

Do field research on that cyberspace. Document this/these space(s). Collect urls, take screenshots, make notes.

How do adolescents see themselves, use/appropriate this/these space(s)? How do they find/lose/interrogate their identity in this/these space(s)?

## Fictionalization
Based on this field research, how can you transform the interface/exerience into your own fictionalized world, adapted to the story you want to tell?

This fictionalized form is a sort of abstraction of the actual interface/world you discovered in your field research.

Think about what you keep and what you remove from your referenced world you studied in your field research.
