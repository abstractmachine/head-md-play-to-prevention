# head-md-play-to-prevention
Play To Prevention is an Innosuisse-funded research project developed in collaboration with the [Master Media Design](https://head.hesge.ch/en/programs-research/master-arts-media-design) at [HEAD – Genève](https://head.hesge.ch) ([HES-SO](https://hesge.ch)) and [Arpège](https://arpegespt.ch).

## Research
We are currently at the research phase of the project. During this phase, we will be investigating various documents related to cyberbullying.
